## GLOBALE STYLE-DIREKTIVE (bei JEDEM Prompt einfügen)

```
REALISM RULES — apply to every scene:
- Shot on iPhone selfie camera, NOT a professional camera
- Slightly shaky, handheld — no gimbal, no smooth movements
- Natural window light or bathroom ceiling light — no studio lighting, no ring light glow
- Background: real lived-in space (products on shelf, towels hanging, mirror smudges)
- NO perfect lighting, NO color grading, NO cinematic filters
- Compression artifacts okay — looks like a real TikTok video
- Avatar: no heavy makeup, natural skin texture visible (pores, slight redness okay)
- Clothes: oversized hoodie, old t-shirt, or tank top — nothing stylized
- Hair at start: genuinely messy, frizzy, or just woken up — NOT styled
- Speech: slightly hesitant, natural pauses, authentic German — NOT read from script
- Reactions: genuine, slightly awkward — NOT polished influencer energy
```

## AD 06R — "300€ GESPART" (Rewrite)
**Hook:** Price shock — stärkster rationaler Kaufgrund

```
Mode: UGC — iPhone selfie style
Avatar: Woman, 27-34, no makeup, old oversized hoodie,
        standing in her bathroom, products visible on shelf behind her
Language: German (Deutsch)
Duration: 15 seconds

REALISM RULES (see above — apply all)

[0-3s] HOOK — She holds up her phone showing a screenshot of a salon price,
  looks directly into camera with eyebrows raised, genuine shock:
  "Dreihundert Euro. Für Keratin. Im Salon."
  Slight pause. "Ich hab das jetzt für 75 gemacht."
[3-9s] She gestures at herself, runs hand through smooth hair:
  "Das ist Smuuve. Vegane Keratin-Behandlung, zuhause.
   Einmal alle 3 Monate — und ich brauch meinen Friseur nicht mehr dafür."
  [Camera wobbles slightly as she grabs Smuuve bottle from shelf]
[9-12s] She holds bottle toward camera — use uploaded product image here:
  "74,95€. Ernsthaft."
  Small laugh, shakes head.
[12-15s] Looks back at camera:
  "Link steht unten."

Use uploaded product reference image for the bottle. Do NOT generate the bottle label.
Background: Real bathroom shelf with products, slightly messy
NO color grading. NO ring light. Natural ceiling or window light only.
```

## Wichtigste Realism-Checks vor Upload

- [ ] Kein Ring Light / kein Studio-Licht sichtbar?
- [ ] Hintergrund leicht unordentlich / nicht staged?
- [ ] Avatar hat sichtbare Poren, kein heavy makeup?
- [ ] Kamera leicht wackelig / nicht perfectly smooth?
- [ ] Echtes Produktfoto eingebunden (kein generiertes Label)?
- [ ] Sprechweise klingt nicht abgelesen?
