## 9. Daily Monitoring Queries (Shopify Analytics)

Im Shopify Admin → Analytics → Reports → Custom Reports. Diese drei jeden Morgen 08:00 checken:

```
Daily Sales (gestern):
FROM sales SHOW orders, gross_sales, average_order_value WHERE date = yesterday

Traffic Source Breakdown (letzte 7d):
FROM sessions SHOW sessions, conversion_rate GROUP BY referrer_source SINCE -7d

Top-Performer-Produkt (letzte 7d):
FROM sales SHOW orders, gross_sales GROUP BY product_title ORDER BY orders DESC SINCE -7d
```

Wenn du den `smuuve-scale` Aggregator deployst (siehe anderes Repo), läuft das automatisch um 07:00 in Slack/Telegram.
